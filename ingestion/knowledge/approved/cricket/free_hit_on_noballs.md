# Free Hit Regulations for No-Balls in Limited Overs

Under ICC limited-overs playing conditions, the delivery immediately following any no-ball (whether called for front-foot overstepping or fielding circle breaches) is designated as a Free Hit for the batting side.

During a Free Hit delivery:
1. The striker cannot be dismissed by any method credited to the bowler (Bowled, Caught, LBW, Stumped, Hit Wicket).
2. The batter can only be dismissed by fielding-based infractions: Run Out, Obstructing the Field, or Hitting the Ball Twice.
3. Field changes are not permitted for the Free Hit delivery unless the batters changed ends during the preceding no-ball delivery or the no-ball resulted from a fielding restriction error.

If the delivery designated as a Free Hit is itself another illegal delivery (wide or no-ball), the penalty run is added and the Free Hit status rolls over to the subsequent delivery.
