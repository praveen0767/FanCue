# Wides and No-Balls as Illegal Deliveries

Wides and no-balls are illegal deliveries defined under Laws 21 and 22 of the MCC Laws of Cricket. They impose direct scoring and ball-counting penalties on the bowling side.

A wide is called when the ball passes out of the batter's reach and where the batter could not make contact from a normal stance. A no-ball is called for bowler infractions including overstepping the popping crease with the front foot, breaking the return crease, or bowling dangerous high full-toss deliveries above the waist.

Both infractions award at least one automatic penalty run to the batting team (recorded under extras), and the delivery does not count as one of the six legal balls in the over. Any additional physical runs completed by the batters off a wide are recorded as wides, while runs scored from the bat off a no-ball are credited to the batter. Furthermore, in limited-overs formats, a no-ball results in a Free Hit on the immediately following delivery.
