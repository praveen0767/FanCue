# Batting Partnerships and Individual Scoring Rates

A batting partnership measures the cumulative runs scored by the batting team while a specific pair of batters occupies the crease between dismissals.

The first partnership begins at the start of the innings (0/0) and ends upon the fall of the first wicket. Subsequent partnerships are designated by the wicket number (e.g., the 3rd-wicket partnership measures runs scored between the fall of the 2nd and 3rd wickets). A partnership comprises runs scored off the bat by both batters plus all extras conceded during their tenure.

An individual batter's scoring rate is expressed as a Strike Rate (runs scored divided by legal balls faced, multiplied by 100). A bowler's performance rate is measured as Economy Rate (total runs conceded divided by legal overs bowled). Examining partnership runs alongside individual strike rates reveals which partner drove the scoring and how pressure was absorbed.
