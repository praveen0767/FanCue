# ICC Men's ODI Fielding Restrictions and Powerplays

Under ICC Men's ODI Playing Conditions, field placements are divided into three distinct phases termed Powerplays (P1, P2, and P3) during a standard 50-over innings.

1. **Powerplay 1 (Overs 1 to 10):** Mandatory first powerplay. A maximum of two fielding players are permitted outside the 30-yard fielding circle at the instant of delivery.
2. **Powerplay 2 (Overs 11 to 40):** Middle overs phase. A maximum of four fielding players are permitted outside the 30-yard circle.
3. **Powerplay 3 (Overs 41 to 50):** Death overs phase. A maximum of five fielding players are permitted outside the 30-yard circle.

These restrictions dynamically dictate boundary protection and scoring ease. If the fielding captain positions more fielders outside the circle than permitted by the active Powerplay, the delivery is signaled as a No-ball.
