# One Day International (ODI) Match Structure

A Men's One Day International (ODI) is a limited-overs match format played between two national teams with official ODI status, governed by the ICC Men's ODI Playing Conditions.

The standard match comprises two innings of up to 50 overs each (300 legal deliveries per innings), scheduled across a single day. The team winning the toss elects either to bat first or bowl first. The first batting side sets a cumulative run target. The second batting side attempts to surpass that target before their 50 overs expire or 10 wickets are lost.

If match overs are reduced due to weather or interruptions, targets in the second innings are adjusted using the Duckworth-Lewis-Stern (DLS) method. An uninterrupted match concludes when the chasing team reaches the target, is bowled all out, or exhausts 50 overs.
