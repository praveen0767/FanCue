# Bat Runs versus Total Runs

Every run recorded in cricket contributes to the batting team's total score, but runs are partitioned into batter runs and extra runs (extras).

Bat runs are runs scored off the batter's bat or hand/glove holding the bat. These runs are credited directly to the individual striker's personal score and count toward milestones such as half-centuries and centuries. Total runs comprise all bat runs plus all extras incurred by the fielding team (wides, no-balls, byes, leg-byes, and penalty runs).

When analyzing a match interval, the total runs added reflects the overall team score change, whereas individual batter tallies isolate who drove that scoring. Deliveries where extras occurred add runs to the team total without inflating the striker's individual personal tally.
