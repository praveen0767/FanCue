# Decision Review System (DRS) Protocols in International Cricket

The Decision Review System (DRS) allows on-field player review of specific umpire decisions regarding dismissals, utilizing television broadcast technology and ball-tracking systems.

Each team is allocated two unsuccessful reviews per innings in One Day Internationals. A player review must be signaled within 15 seconds of the on-field decision. The Third Umpire reviews ball-tracking trajectories, ultra-edge audio spikes, and side-on cameras to determine whether the original on-field decision should be upheld, reversed, or retained under "Umpire's Call".

When ball-tracking indicates an Umpire's Call on impact or hitting the stumps, the original on-field decision stands, but the reviewing team does not forfeit their review allocation. DRS reviews frequently cause pauses in play and alter the dismissal record within an interval.
