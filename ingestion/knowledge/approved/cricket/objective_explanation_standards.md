# Factual Interval Reporting without Invented Narratives

A central design mandate of FanCue is that interval explanations must be strictly grounded in observable match facts rather than speculative tactical commentary or unprovable psychological claims.

Factual reporting focuses on demonstrable events:
1. Exact runs added and wickets lost during the selected boundaries.
2. Identifiable deliveries where boundaries, extras, or dismissals occurred.
3. Quantifiable metrics such as interval run rate, maiden overs bowled, and strike distribution.
4. Documented bowler changes and fielding restrictions in effect.

Language models must never assert that a player "felt nervous", "lost focus", or "succumbed to pressure" unless an authoritative referee or official source recorded that specific finding. Explaining an interval means clarifying the mechanics of what happened on the field using verified evidence.
