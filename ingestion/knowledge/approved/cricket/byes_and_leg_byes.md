# Byes and Leg-Byes

Byes and leg-byes are categories of extras conceded without the delivery being deemed illegal. Unlike wides and no-balls, deliveries resulting in byes or leg-byes count as legal balls toward completing the over.

A bye occurs when a fair delivery passes the striker without contacting either the bat or the batter's person, and the batters successfully run or the ball reaches the boundary. A leg-bye occurs when the ball deflects off the batter's person or protective equipment (other than the glove holding the bat), provided the batter either attempted to play the ball with the bat or tried to evade being struck. If no shot was offered and no evasive action was taken, leg-byes cannot be scored and the umpire calls dead ball.

Neither byes nor leg-byes are credited to the individual batter's score, and neither are debited against the bowler's personal bowling analysis in standard match scorecards.
