# Per-Bowler Over Quotas in Limited Overs Matches

In limited-overs international cricket, no single bowler may bowl more than 20% of the scheduled overs allocated to an innings.

In an uninterrupted 50-over One Day International, this rule establishes a strict individual ceiling of 10 completed overs per bowler. In a standard 20-over T20 international, the ceiling is 4 overs per bowler. A fielding team must utilize at least five separate bowlers to complete the full 50-over innings allocation.

If a match is shortened due to rain or stoppages, individual quotas reduce proportionately. Understanding bowler quotas provides context for bowling changes in an interval, such as when a captain must withhold a frontline strike bowler's remaining overs for the final death-overs phase.
