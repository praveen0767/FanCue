# Super Over Tie-Break Procedure

A Super Over is a one-over-per-side tie-breaking procedure utilized in knockout stages of limited-overs tournaments when aggregate match scores finish tied at the conclusion of standard overs.

In a Super Over, each team bats for a single six-ball over, nominating three batters (two wickets permitted) and one designated bowler. The team with higher runs at the conclusion of both single-over innings wins the fixture. If the Super Over itself terminates in a tie, successive Super Overs are played until an outright winner is determined.
